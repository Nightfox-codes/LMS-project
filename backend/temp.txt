# Instructors with their courses
GET  /api/instructors-overview   → [{id, firstname, lastname, email, courses:[{id,title,status}]}]

# Modules
GET    /api/courses/<id>/modules
POST   /api/courses/<id>/modules

# Lessons
GET    /api/modules/<id>/lessons

PUT    /api/lessons/<id>
DELETE /api/lessons/<id>

# Materials
